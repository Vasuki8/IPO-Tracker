Trust, repeat usefulness, accessibility, discoverability, dependable operation and sustainable costs are standing requirements. This register is a work plan, not legal clearance or evidence of demand.

## Customer/value validation before billing

Candidate segments, **hypotheses only**: self-directed Indian IPO researchers who revisit several issues during a bidding week; small independent research teams that repeatedly reconcile filings. Neither is selected as the paying audience. The proposed recurring problem is reconciling source facts, changes and deadlines without mistaking provisional or disputed data for final figures.

Candidate differentiation: source-linked, field-specific evidence; explicit review holds; clear source clocks; repeatable comparison and saved research. Candidate free offering: discoverable company pages, basic calendar and transparent methodology. Candidate paid value to investigate later: reliable watchlist workflows, research exports or verified event alerts. Alert delivery, financial comparisons and exports have data-rights and quality prerequisites. No pricing, subscription demand or conversion rate has been validated.

After owner approval for outreach, use short task-based interviews/demos with both candidate segments. Record current workaround, repeated task frequency, an observed successful research task, return intent, concrete willingness-to-pay evidence and support expectations. Do not count compliments or a hypothetical price question as a sale. Select an initial segment/offering only when repeated problems and actual return/use evidence justify it; do not build billing while the result is unknown.

## Measurement plan — not activated

Proposed events: directory discovery, profile opened, comparison completed, watchlist action, return to research, and (only after an offering exists) conversion. Start with aggregate counts and coarse time windows. Do not transmit search text, watchlist contents, financial holdings, account identifiers or fingerprinting signals. Anonymous event totals cannot reliably measure unique people or return visits; decide whether a consented, short-lived identifier is justified before measuring those metrics.

Before any telemetry, document operator/audience jurisdictions, hosting logs, processors, fields, purpose, retention, access, consent/opt-out requirements and the privacy notice. Current local watchlist storage is not a promise that the host has no server logs. This milestone introduces no analytics SDK, cookie banner workaround, account database or outbound tracking.

## Cost and architecture decisions

Keep source adapters, canonical records/review history, public projections and future private account/entitlement data separate. Static/light presentation remains the implementation; a commercial hosting decision is pending, not an argument for a broad application rewrite.

Track actual Actions minutes/artifact storage, public egress/build size, data-license quotes, domain/hosting charges and operator review/support time. Account invoices and negotiated feed prices were not inspected; current total operating cost is **unknown**, not asserted to be zero. No new spending, contracts or paid plans were activated. Before migration, compare permission fit, expected usage/egress, recurring cost, rollback and URL continuity with at least one static-host alternative; obtain approval for the selected plan.

## Public operations and professional-review gates

A correction report should identify the stable company URL, field, claimed value/date, source page and observation time, without personal financial information. Engineering triages source failure, undisclosed information, unsupported extraction, identity conflict and conflicting evidence separately. Preserve previous values/proofs and the reason for every resolution; publish corrections only after source acceptance and verify the deployed page/export. Do not promise a response SLA until support capacity is selected.

Recovery requires a known accepted canonical commit, reproducible public build, retained source/correction artifacts and a restore rehearsal. If a displayed fact is disputed, a source/value-bound public hold may contain the incident without erasing evidence. Unrelated collector or canonical rollbacks require explicit review. Automated overdue-update detection, proposal triage and recovery rehearsal remain roadmap work, not completed by a successful UI build.

Before monetizing affected features, qualified review must determine the actual operator and target jurisdictions; whether the offered research/analysis, recommendations or personalization trigger financial-information/adviser/research-analyst obligations; privacy/consumer obligations; processor/cross-border arrangements; payment, cancellation and tax requirements; and rights for each redistributed source. These are unresolved questions, not a claim of exemption based on a disclaimer.

Sponsorship or paid placement must be identifiable and cannot affect source verification, factual values or research ordering. No advertisements or sponsor arrangements have been activated.
