This is a public commercial product in development. Paying audience, pricing and
revenue model are undecided. Customer trust, source rights, accessible mobile
research, privacy and sustainable costs remain standing requirements. Keep the

changes without owner approval. Sponsorship cannot influence verified facts or
research rankings.
