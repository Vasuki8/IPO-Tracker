## Support, corrections and incidents

Owner is the decision point until a support operator/channel is selected. Proposed intake: company URL, field/value, period/unit, official document/page and observation time. Never request holdings, PAN or bank details. Public GitHub issues are public disclosure; choose an appropriate channel before inviting sensitive reports. No new contact/form/SLA activated.

Triage source unavailable, disclosure absent, parser unsupported, evidence/identity conflicts, stale observation, failed collection, unresolved proposal, publication delay and rights/privacy complaints separately. Prioritize misleading active terms, identity errors and rights/privacy incidents; assign owner, preserve evidence/clocks and state impact. Existing source/value-bound holds contain factual disputes without erasing history. Rights-related publication restrictions need owner/professional direction and lawful evidence retention, separate from factual correctness holds.

Resolve via source review, bounded correction, generation, regressions, deployment and live/profile/comparison/export checks. Retain old/new values, source hashes/pages, reasons, reviewer/version and release ID. External notifications require an approved channel/process. Rehearse recovery from a known accepted commit before promising recovery targets.

The [operational report](UPDATE_HEALTH.md) and PRs #149–150 already distinguish failed/deferred outcomes, unknown observations, proposal ages and publication windows. They are not always-on alerts, staffed response or a completed recovery rehearsal. Missing historical clocks/blocked feeds remain in PROJECT_STATUS; do not duplicate the tooling.
