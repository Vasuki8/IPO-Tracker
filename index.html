DATA DESIGN

Official sources have different jobs.

01

SEBI discovery

DRHP, UDRHP, RHP and final prospectus filings create the pre-exchange IPO pipeline and retain official document links.

02

NSE spine

Current, upcoming, detail and historical exchange records remain the primary source for issue dates and exchange fields.

03

BSE validation

BSE fills missing issue terms and independently checks comparable fields such as dates, price band, lot size and issue size.

04

SEBI document extraction

Abridged RHP/Prospectus PDFs are parsed for fresh issue and OFS composition, lead managers, registrar, promoters, objects and restated financials.

05

Exchange subscription history

QIB, NII/HNI, Retail/Individual and Total demand is captured throughout the live bidding window. NSE is preferred, with BSE's official cumulative-demand schedule as the cloud-runner fallback.

06

Completeness repair queue

Lifecycle-aware audits identify missing official fields and prioritize open/upcoming IPOs before historical backfills.
