OUR APPROACH

Know where the numbers come from.

Source links, timestamps and disagreements stay attached to the company record.

01

Regulatory filings

SEBI filings help identify the pipeline. DRHP, RHP and prospectus links stay available for further research.

02

Exchange issue data

NSE supplies issue dates and terms. BSE fills gaps and provides an independent comparison where values are comparable.

03

Offer documents

Available official documents support issue composition, financials, promoters, use of proceeds and intermediaries.

04

Transparent coverage

Missing values stay empty. Conflicting source values remain visible. Coverage checks prioritize current issues.
