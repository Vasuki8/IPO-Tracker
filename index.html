DATA & SOURCES

Know what’s behind a number.

IPO Tracker presents published information from official filings and disclosures. Coverage is incomplete and continues to grow.

Verified
A field is supported by retained official evidence.
Provisional
The published field is marked provisional. Review the supporting source.
Conflict
Retained sources disagree. Review the evidence before relying on the value.
Missing
The value is unavailable. A dash never means zero.

What does “Partial” mean?

Directory evidence covers six terms: price or band, lot size, issue size, opening, closing and listing dates. “Partial” means at least one is missing; it does not mean the other figures lack sources. “Complete” means all six are verified.

Different dates, different meanings

A source publication date is when a document was issued. Collection time is when its information was retrieved. Dataset generation is when the published dataset was assembled; it is not a claim that every source was checked then.

The year filter uses the opening date, then closing or listing date when necessary. A final Prospectus is not required for an IPO to appear.
